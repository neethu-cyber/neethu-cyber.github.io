Why did 1,000 homes spend 9% more on electricity than planned?

How I work

Start from the decision

A forecast or a dashboard is useful when it helps someone decide. In my medicine project, the most accurate forecast did not lead to the best orders.

Check the data first

My pipelines test for missing hours, impossible values and totals that don't reconcile. At Siemens I traced gaps in carbon reporting back to their source.

Make it easy to use

I build for the people who read the numbers: Power BI reports for finance teams, a live dashboard, and READMEs anyone can follow.
